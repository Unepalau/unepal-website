# uNepal app UI capture inventory

These assets were captured from the installed uNepal Android app, version 5.0.5 (build 131), on a connected physical device on 9 August 2026. The source application repository was used read-only.

## Approved public captures

- `home-feed.webp` and `home-composer.webp`: public home feed and composer surface.
- `community-feed.webp` and `community-post.webp`: public Bagmati community posts.
- `groups-directory.webp`: public community group feed.
- `bazaar-marketplace.webp` and `bazaar-listing.webp`: public Bazaar listing.
- `hamro-tv.webp` and `hamro-tv-grid.webp`: public Hamro TV catalogue.
- `business-directory.webp`: public business directory.
- `nepali-calendar-events.webp` and `calendar-event.webp`: Nepali calendar and public event.

The captures contain only public or controlled demo-like content visible in public app surfaces. No email addresses, phone numbers, direct messages, notifications, private account data, or private media are included.

## Explicit exclusions

- The menu capture was rejected because it exposed an account name and administrator role.
- Messages and notifications were not opened or captured.
- A failed Pages-directory capture showing the Android launcher was rejected.
- Blank/transient captures were rejected.

Do not replace these files with captures from authenticated private surfaces without a new privacy review.
