# Legal and policy wording requiring owner review

The website implementation deliberately does not change the following policy promises or legal positions. Each item needs confirmation from the appropriate product, operations, or legal owner before the public policy text is revised.

## Product terminology in policies

- Community Guidelines and Privacy Policy still refer to “Circles”. The current app uses “Groups”. Confirm whether this is a straightforward terminology correction or whether “Circles” remains a legally defined legacy feature.
- Community Guidelines describe listings as part of “Explore”. The current app presents jobs, services, housing, rentals, items, and requests through the unified Bazaar area.
- Community Guidelines list Rashifal, Forex, and calculators. Current app source directly verifies the Nepali Calendar but did not verify those other tools.
- Terms include an astrology/Rashifal disclaimer even though current public support was not verified.

## Operational promises

- Community Guidelines promise priority action on valid child-safety and serious-abuse reports within 24 hours.
- Community Guidelines state that users can appeal a removal or restriction within 14 days.
- Community Guidelines include permanent device-level bans and escalation to venues, platforms, or authorities.
- Child Safety Standards contain review, removal, escalation, detection, reporting, and response commitments that should match current moderation staffing and procedures.

## Geographic and regulatory scope

- Listing rules contain Australia-specific concepts including suburb/state fields, rent bidding, minimum pay, visa rights, and right-to-let checks, while the product is positioned for Nepalese communities worldwide.
- Terms and Privacy Policy contain Australian regulatory and governing-law language. Confirm how it should apply to users in Nepal and other countries.

## Store and release wording

- Android 5.0.5 is publicly available.
- iOS 5.0.5 must not be described as publicly available until the public App Store listing changes from 5.0.4.
- The App Store privacy compatibility URL should remain supported through a `/privacy.html` to `/privacy` redirect.
